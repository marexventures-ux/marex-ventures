Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Marex Ventures

1. Information We Collect

When you book a consultation, submit a discovery call inquiry, or contact us through our website, we may collect your name, work email address, phone or WhatsApp number, company name, team size, industry, and project details. We use this information solely to communicate with you and evaluate your business requirements.

2. Client Data & Confidentiality

As a custom software, automation, and AI systems provider, we treat all client business processes, workflows, and proprietary data with strict confidentiality. During training programs and system buildouts, company data is handled using secure, client-controlled environments. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your company data under any circumstances.

3. Safe AI Practices

In our corporate training programs (including AI Without Code), we explicitly instruct teams on responsible AI adoption. We train organizations to establish internal AI usage guidelines ensuring sensitive customer information and proprietary trade secrets are never shared with public or unverified AI models.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We may use standard web analytics and ad tracking technologies (such as Google Analytics or Meta/LinkedIn pixels) to analyze website traffic and measure campaign performance. These tools collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage statistics.
